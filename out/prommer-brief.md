# Counterparty brief: Thomas Prommer

## Who

Thomas Prommer is a technology executive and AI strategist, Group CEO of We The Flywheel. He previously served as Global SVP Engineering at Adidas, CIO at Sweetgreen, and President, Technology at Huge.

## Evidence

- At Adidas, led an organisation of 500+ engineers powering a $9B+ e-commerce operation across 50 markets — prommer.net.
- At Huge (IPG), grew the technology practice from 50 to 500+ engineers across 14 global offices, delivering engagements for Apple, Google, McDonald's, Toyota, and Nike — prommer.net.
- Named HotTopics Global CIO 2026, a peer-nominated, independently judged recognition of the world's most influential digital, technology, and AI leaders — USA Today press release (prommer.net Newsroom), 22 June 2026.
- Quoted as Global SVP Engineering at Adidas on three fixes for the AI accountability gap: named technical and business sponsors co-owning outcomes, embedded AI squads replacing a central Center of Excellence, and stage-gated outcome funding — CIO.inc (ISMG), 1 July 2026.
- Gave three sourced recommendations on AI ROI — joint project-level accountability, embedded AI squads over centralized CoEs, and stage-gated outcome funding — CIO.com, 1 June 2026.
- Two sourced quotes as former President at Huge: enterprises stick to low-risk copilot tools to sidestep model-risk governance, and transformation lands only when a CEO, investor, or single P&L owner drives it with accountability — Okoone, 24 June 2026.
- Quoted on mandatory AI attribution backfiring in engineering teams at Adidas: "They didn't want their best contributions footnoted as 'cowritten by Claude'" — Business Insider, 13 July 2026.
- Argues identity verification should not end after an initial login, and that trust should evolve continuously with user behavior and risk — VC Magazine, 17 July 2026.
- Featured as a case study — marathoner and triathlete who used a GLP-1 medication after four months of injury layoff — in an investigative piece on GLP-1 use in sport — Sportschau (ARD), 3 September 2026.
